Founded in 1999, Jaimini Public School has been a beacon of academic excellence and character development in Hiriyur, Chitradurga district. We combine rigorous curriculum with a nurturing environment — because grades alone do not define a child's future.

🎓

CBSE Curriculum with IB Enrichment

Nationally recognized board curriculum enhanced with international best practices, project-based learning, and critical thinking modules.

🏆

Holistic Development

Beyond textbooks — sports, arts, robotics, debate, and 50+ co-curricular activities to shape well-rounded human beings.

🤝

Inclusive & Safe Environment

A zero-tolerance anti-bullying policy, counselling support, and an open-door culture where every student feels seen and valued.

Join Us
Begin your Jaimini journey

Admissions are open for Classes VI to XII for the 2025–26 academic year. We follow a transparent, merit-based process. Seats are limited — apply early.

1

Fill the Online Form

Complete the inquiry form with basic student details, preferred class, and contact information.

2

Attend the Entrance Assessment

A 60-minute aptitude test followed by a brief parent and student interaction with our academic counsellor.

3

Receive Your Offer Letter

Results communicated within 5 working days. Selected candidates receive a formal offer letter via email.

4

Complete Enrollment

Submit required documents, pay the admission fee, and collect your welcome kit. Welcome to Jaimini Public School!
